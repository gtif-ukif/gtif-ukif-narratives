# Food Security: Drought Occurrence and Impact on Food Systems <!--{ as="image" mode="hero" src="https://gtif-ukif.github.io/gtif-ukif-narratives/assets/previews/food_systems/rgb_albedo_east_anglia_aug_2022_hero.png" }-->
### A look at Drought occurrence and impact on Food Systems across the UK, Ireland & France <!--{ style="font-size:1rem;opacity:0.8;margin-top:1rem;" }-->

## Overview

A 10m spectral true colour (Red Green Blue) mosaic albedo map of central and East England for a non-drought year (2020) and a year of severe drought (2022) derived from Sentinel-2 using VIIRS BRDF and the EU Copernicus Land Monitoring system for the Sentinel-2 Global Mosaic. For 2022, a NDVI has been calculated from the spectral BHR albedos in Red and NIR. Tracking the NDVI across months and between years enables us to uncover the trigger dates for the drought.
