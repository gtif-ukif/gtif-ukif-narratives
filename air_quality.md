# Mobility: Urban Air Quality (PM2.5) at a Street Level <!--{ as="image" mode="hero" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/eurodatacube/eodash-assets/main/stories/covid-no2/hero.jpg" }-->
### A look at urban air quality at a street level, to encourage active travel <!--{ style="font-size:1rem;opacity:0.8;margin-top:1rem;" }-->

## Overview

A 1 km daily map of the UKIF area and a zoom on London, derived from the MODIS-MAIC product, are shown in the two animations below for 5 years (2018-01-01 to 2022-12-31).

Forty-eight high exceedance incidents are shown for London, with similar numbers of incidents in France. Outside the UKIF area, the Benelux countries and the Po Valley show a high number of PM2.5 incidents.

It should be noted that the WHO "safe limit" for PM2.5 is 10 ug/cu.m. Many of these incidents show exceedances above 50 ug/cu.m.

Single Image Super- Resolution (SISR) has been applied to generate 250m PM2.5 maps from 2017-22. All the published in situ PM2.5 have been added so you can plot time series of MODIS vs in situ as well as correlation plots by site (click on site). Road infrastructure based on the OSM project is included as well as road and air traffic densities so you can visualise the link between mobility by fossil fuels and PM2.5.
